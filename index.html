来源与方法

这页不把梦当成事实，也不把任何一条解释当成唯一答案。条目综合了三类公开传统：古代梦书、精神分析，以及现代民俗型梦境词典中的常见说法。

  • Artemidorus，《Oneirocritica》（约公元 2 世纪）
  • Sigmund Freud，《The Interpretation of Dreams》（1900）
  • C. G. Jung，《Collected Works》第 8、9i、12 卷
  • 常见民俗母题与现代梦境词典，仅用于对照，不照搬原文

传统之间会互相矛盾。这里写“常见解读”，是说明它属于哪种解释传统，并非宣称它对你一定成立。

This page treats neither dreams nor any single reading as a fact. The entries summarise recurring interpretations from three public traditions: early dream manuals, psychoanalysis, and modern folk dream dictionaries.

  • Artemidorus, Oneirocritica (2nd century CE)
  • Sigmund Freud, The Interpretation of Dreams (1900)
  • C. G. Jung, Collected Works, volumes 8, 9i, and 12
  • Common folk motifs and modern dream dictionaries, used for comparison rather than copied

Traditions disagree. “A common reading” names a tradition; it does not claim that the reading is true for you.
